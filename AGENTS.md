# Global preferences

## Spelling
Use British English spellings (e.g. colour, behaviour, organise, licence as a noun, initialise, optimise).

## Comments
Comments can have as many sentences as appropriate, but each sentence must be under 40 words, to comply with SonarQube's comment-length rule.

## Punctuation
Never use the em dash (—) anywhere, in code, comments, commit messages, or prose. Use a comma, a colon, a semicolon, or split into separate sentences instead.
